
T5 PINN Training Summary
===================================

Task: PINN for 1D Poisson Equation

1. Problem
-u''(x) = pi^2 * sin(pi*x)
Domain: 0 < x < 1
Boundary conditions: u(0) = u(1) = 0
Exact solution: u(x) = sin(pi*x)

2. Model Configuration
Network: MLP (1 -> 32 -> 32 -> 1)
Activation: Tanh
Optimizer: Adam
Learning rate: 0.001
Epochs: 3000
Interior collocation points: 100
Boundary points: 2
Random seed: 42

3. Loss Functions
PDE loss: Mean squared PDE residual
BC loss: Mean squared boundary error
Total loss = PDE loss + BC loss

4. Experimental Observations
The total loss decreased during training.
The PDE loss decreased to approximately 2e-5.
The BC loss decreased to approximately 1e-9.
The PINN prediction was close to the exact solution.
The maximum absolute error was approximately 9e-5.
